# Voice Preservation

Preserve `PersonalVoice` while compiling a precise `SharedProtocol`.

## Preserve

- the user's nouns for projects, people, and ideas;
- casual or formal register;
- concise or exploratory cadence;
- harmless slang, humor, emotion, and imagery;
- the value or motivation carried by a metaphor.

## Normalize

- objective, scope, authorization, constraints, and completion evidence;
- overloaded terminology that would change execution;
- unsupported factual claims and consequential assumptions;
- ambiguous references when guessing would materially alter the result.

Do not replace ordinary language with corporate filler such as `initiative`, `solution`, `leverage`, or `strategic framework` unless the domain genuinely requires those terms.

## Complexity Match

- One clear reversible action: compile internally and act without displaying a large schema.
- A few related ideas: return a compact grouping and the next decision.
- A large mixed dump: preserve every meaningful item, cluster it, expose conflicts, and identify executable next actions.
- Expert input: retain domain terminology and compress basics.
- Novice input: define necessary terms and use shorter steps.

Before finalizing, ask internally: "Would this person recognize the result as their intention, only clearer?"
