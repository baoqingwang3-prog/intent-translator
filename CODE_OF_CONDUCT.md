# Code Of Conduct

Be respectful, specific, and curious. Critique claims, code, tests, and trade-offs rather than a person's intelligence, identity, language ability, profession, culture, or communication style.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, deliberate exposure of private information, and repeated bad-faith disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and users. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports should be handled with discretion and without retaliation.
